# Diagnosia module overview

Diagnosia has three core tools and four add-ons. This describes what each one does in a workflow. It gives no clinical advice.

## Core tools

- **DiagnoFast** takes a patient's history by a spoken interview in the waiting room, through a single-use link, and prepares a report for the doctor.
- **Skin Cancer Detection** is a research screening aid for doctors. It isn't a diagnosis or an approved medical device.
- **Prescription Analyzer** reads a prescription photo. The patient confirms each medicine before anything is explained.

## Add-ons

- **ClinScribe** turns a consultation into a draft note. A clinician signs it. It drafts from the transcript alone.
- **LabLens** reads lab reports into results that a person confirms.
- **DischargeCare** turns a discharge plan into a family checklist and team tasks.
- **ReferralLoop** follows a referral from request to returned outcome.

OptiKinesis is a separate accessibility companion, not a clinical add-on.
