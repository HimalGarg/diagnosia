# Approved terminology

Words used across Diagnosia, so agents and people mean the same thing.

- **Workflow reference:** a generated reference such as a case reference. It identifies a workflow without identifying a person.
- **Handoff:** one tool making confirmed information available to another. It carries references only.
- **Generated:** an AI produced something. Nobody has checked it yet.
- **Reviewed:** a person has looked at it.
- **Approved or signed:** an authorized person has accepted it.
- **Activity view:** a read-only timeline. It is never the official state.
- **Unsure:** the reader isn't confident. It must go to a person.
- **Unreadable:** the reader couldn't read it. It must not be guessed or explained.
