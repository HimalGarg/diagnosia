Frequently asked questions

What is Diagnosia?

A healthcare platform for India with AI tools on one patient record. It follows a patient from the waiting room to long-term care, gives doctors back their time, and helps patients understand their care.

Which tools are there, and who are they for?

Before the visit: DiagnoFast. During it: ClinScribe, LabLens, MedGuard and Skin Cancer Detection. After it: Prescription Analyzer, ReferralLoop, DischargeCare and ChronicCare. For long-term care: MaternaCare and AMR Guard. OptiKinesis is for anyone who can't use their hands. Each tool shows you only what fits your account.

Do I need a separate account for each tool?

No. One sign-in opens everything, and the patient you have open stays with you as you move between tools. Doctors and other clinic staff are verified by their organization before clinical tools open. Patients answering a DiagnoFast interview need no account at all.

How does DiagnoFast work?

Reception creates a single-use link for each patient. The patient answers spoken questions in Hindi or English while they wait, or types instead. It fills in the doctor's own form and writes a one-page report. Urgent symptoms such as chest pain stop the interview and send the patient to reception. It never diagnoses.

What does ClinScribe do with my consultation?

With the patient's consent, it transcribes the consultation and drafts a note. It flags anything the transcript doesn't support, and nothing reaches the record until a clinician signs. The audio isn't kept.

How do LabLens and MedGuard help?

LabLens reads a lab report and shows each result beside the original, so a person can confirm it. MedGuard lines up medicine lists to find shared ingredients and allergy matches. Its interaction rules stay off until a pharmacist has reviewed them, and it tells you what it didn't check.

How accurate is the skin cancer detection?

It's a research demo, not a diagnosis, and not an approved medical device. On 3,619 unseen images it sent 90.1% of melanomas for referral, and also 42.7% of harmless lesions, so it's a cautious screener rather than a precise classifier. A doctor's judgement and a biopsy are still needed.

What do I need for the Prescription Analyzer?

A clear photo of your prescription, or up to four if it has several sides. You confirm each medicine, then it explains how to take it and what to eat and avoid, in Hindi or English. It never changes a dose. Anything it can't read goes on a list for your pharmacist.

What happens after the visit?

ReferralLoop follows a referral until the outcome comes back. DischargeCare gives the family a checklist and the team tracked tasks. ChronicCare tracks readings around a plan your clinician approved. Reminders that aren't confirmed become follow-up work for staff, so nobody is quietly missed.

What are MaternaCare and AMR Guard?

MaternaCare keeps pregnancy and postnatal care in one place and sends every worry to a named person. AMR Guard helps hospital stewardship teams with local antibiograms, culture follow-up and review records. Neither one recommends a treatment.

What is OptiKinesis?

OptiKinesis lets people who can't use their hands control a computer with head movements and blinks, using an ordinary webcam. It's a free beta download for Windows.

Who can see my health information?

Only people on your care team, and you can see who opened your record. Consent is recorded for each purpose, such as AI drafting or sharing a referral, and revoking it takes effect straight away. Stored documents and recordings are encrypted.

Is my information sent to outside services?

Yes, when a tool needs AI. Text, photos and speech go to Groq in the United States, or to Google Gemini when Groq is down, and medicine names go to public drug references. Prescription and skin photos aren't kept. That's why this pilot uses made-up patients only. Our privacy notice has the details.

Can a clinic use Diagnosia with real patients today?

Not yet. Diagnosia is a pilot for synthetic data only. Real patient use needs clinical review, legal and regulatory approval, a data agreement with our AI provider, and an independent security test. The application refuses to accept real patient data until those are recorded.

Does Diagnosia replace my doctor?

No. Every tool supports your doctor's care and never replaces it. Always follow your doctor's advice.

About us

Team Brackets • Delhi Technological University
Builders Himal Garg  •  Khushal Goel  •  Aditya Kumar

In a country of 1.4 billion people, doctor consultations are crushed into brutal two-minute transactions. Overburdened physicians drown in repetitive administrative chaos, while patients leave clutching unreadable prescriptions they can't decode, paralyzed by uncertainty.

Diagnosia dismantles this crisis. Built by Team Brackets at Delhi Technological University, it is an uncompromising, clinical-grade ecosystem uniting 12 specialized AI tools around a single patient timeline. From voice-driven waiting-room triage and ambient clinical transcription to deep dermoscopy screening and instant vernacular prescription decoding with clear dietary guidance—we don't build toy chatbots. We engineer clinical weapons that put hours back into doctors' hands and give patients unequivocal clarity over their care.
