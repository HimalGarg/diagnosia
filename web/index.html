Medical Tools

Four tools that follow a patient's journey: before the consultation, during it, after it, and for anyone who needs hands-free access.

DiagnoFast

Takes the patient's history by voice, in their own language, while they wait, so the doctor starts the consultation with a ready-made report.

Skin Cancer Detection

A screening aid for doctors. Analyses an image of a skin lesion and gives an early second opinion before costly tests such as a biopsy.

Prescription Analyzer

Reads a prescription from a photo and explains it in plain language, with a medicine schedule, diet, yoga and follow-up guidance.

OptiKinesis

Hands-free computer control for people who can't use their hands. Move the cursor with your head and click with a blink, using an ordinary webcam.

Frequently asked questions

What is Diagnosia?

Diagnosia is a healthcare platform with four AI tools: DiagnoFast, Skin Cancer Detection, Prescription Analyzer and OptiKinesis. They help doctors work faster and help patients understand their care.

How accurate is the skin cancer detection?

It's a research demo, not a diagnosis. It gives doctors a second opinion with a confidence score, and a doctor's judgement and a biopsy are still needed.

How does DiagnoFast work?

DiagnoFast asks the patient questions out loud in their own language while they wait, fills in the doctor's custom form from their answers, and gives the doctor a one-page report.

What do I need for the Prescription Analyzer?

Just a clear photo of your prescription. It explains each medicine and when to take it, and what to eat and avoid. It never changes a dose, and if it can't read something it tells you to check with your pharmacist.

What is OptiKinesis?

OptiKinesis lets people who can't use their hands control a computer with head movements and blinks, using an ordinary webcam. It's a free beta download for Windows.

Does Diagnosia replace my doctor?

No. Every tool supports your doctor's care and never replaces it. Always follow your doctor's advice.
