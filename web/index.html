Frequently asked questions

What is Diagnosia?

A healthcare platform for India with seven AI tools on one patient record. It follows a patient from the waiting room through the visit, referral and discharge, gives doctors back their time, and helps patients understand their care.

Which tools are there, and who are they for?

Before the visit: DiagnoFast. During it: ClinScribe, LabLens and Skin Cancer Detection. After it: Prescription Analyzer, ReferralLoop and DischargeCare. OptiKinesis is for anyone who can't use their hands. Each tool shows you only what fits your account.

Do I need a separate account for each tool?

No. One sign-in opens everything, and the patient you have open stays with you as you move between tools. Doctors and other clinic staff are verified by their organization before clinical tools open. Patients answering a DiagnoFast interview need no account at all.

How does DiagnoFast work?

Reception creates a single-use link for each patient. The patient answers spoken questions in Hindi or English while they wait, or types instead. It fills in the doctor's own form and writes a one-page report. Urgent symptoms such as chest pain stop the interview and send the patient to reception. It never diagnoses.

What does ClinScribe do with my consultation?

With the patient's consent, it transcribes the consultation and drafts a note. It flags anything the transcript doesn't support, and nothing reaches the record until a clinician signs. The audio isn't kept.

How does LabLens help?

LabLens reads a lab report and shows each result beside the original, so a person can confirm it. Anything it is unsure of is marked, and nothing reaches the record until a person confirms it.

How accurate is the skin cancer detection?

It's not a diagnosis, and not an approved medical device. On 3,619 unseen images it sent 90.1% of melanomas for referral, and also 42.7% of harmless lesions, so it's a cautious screener rather than a precise classifier. A doctor's judgement and a biopsy are still needed.

What do I need for the Prescription Analyzer?

A clear photo of your prescription, or up to four if it has several sides. You confirm each medicine, then it explains how to take it and what to eat and avoid, in Hindi or English. It never changes a dose. Anything it can't read goes on a list for your pharmacist.

What happens after the visit?

ReferralLoop follows a referral until the outcome comes back. DischargeCare gives the family a checklist and the team tracked tasks. Reminders that aren't confirmed become follow-up work for staff, so nobody is quietly missed.

What is OptiKinesis?

OptiKinesis lets people who can't use their hands control a computer with head movements and blinks, using an ordinary webcam. It's a free beta download for Windows.

Who can see my health information?

Only people on your care team, and you can see who opened your record. Consent is recorded for each purpose, such as AI drafting or sharing a referral, and revoking it takes effect straight away. Stored documents and recordings are encrypted.

Is my information sent to outside services?

Yes, when a tool needs AI. Text, photos and speech go to Groq in the United States, or to Google Gemini when Groq is down,. Prescription and skin photos aren't kept. That's why this pilot uses made-up patients only. Our privacy notice has the details.

Does Diagnosia replace my doctor?

No. Every tool supports your doctor's care and never replaces it. Always follow your doctor's advice.
