# Diagnosia

## Register

product

The workspace is the primary surface. The public site is its front door and should feel like
the same product, not a separate brochure.

## Users

- **Doctors and clinic staff in India**, between patients, often on a shared desktop or tablet.
  They need the right tool for the patient in front of them in one or two clicks.
- **Patients and carers**, often on a phone, with varied reading ability, in English or Hindi.
  They need plain words and one clear next step.
- **Patients in a waiting room**, using a tablet with no account, answering by voice.
- **Clinic administrators and reviewers**, working through governance queues and settings.

## Product purpose

One platform for the whole visit: before it (DiagnoFast), during it (ClinScribe, LabLens,
MedGuard, Skin Cancer Detection), after it (Prescription Analyzer, ReferralLoop, DischargeCare,
ChronicCare), and in long-term programmes (MaternaCare, AMR Guard), plus OptiKinesis for people
who can't use their hands. Success means a person never has to think about which product they
are in.

## Brand personality

Calm, honest, capable. Clinical without being cold. It says what it doesn't know.

## Anti-references

- A brochure on one side and a separate app on the other.
- Tools grouped by when they were built rather than by when they're needed.
- Generic health-tech blue with stock doctors.
- SaaS dashboards full of metric tiles. Clinical work is lists, records and next actions.

## Design principles

1. **One product.** The same name, header, tool names and order everywhere.
2. **Organise by the visit, not by the codebase.**
3. **The patient stays with you.** Moving between tools keeps the open patient.
4. **Unreviewed work looks unreviewed.** AI output is marked until a person checks it.
5. **Always a next step.** Empty screens and refusals say what to do.

## Accessibility and inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Works at phone width, by keyboard, with reduced motion. English and Hindi for
patients. Low-literacy patients use voice, so nothing important is text only.
