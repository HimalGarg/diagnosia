# Diagnosia: Pitch Statistics (MVL '27)

Numbers for the 2-minute video and the pitch deck, with sources. Researched on
26 Oct 2026.

**How to use this file**

- **Hero numbers** (section 0) are the ones to say out loud in the video. Each
  has a ready-made line.
- Everything else is backup for the deck and for judges' questions.
- Every number has a source link. Put a short source line under any number shown
  on screen, e.g. *"Source: BMJ Open, 2017"*.
- Section 10 lists numbers to **avoid** or handle carefully. A judge who catches
  one wrong number stops trusting the rest.
- Wherever possible, quote the newest figure and say the year.

## 10. Numbers to avoid or handle carefully

| Number | Problem | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Doctor-population ratio is 1:811" | It counts AYUSH practitioners and assumes 80% are available. It also weakens your story. | Use 1 government doctor per ~11,000, or say "allopathic-only, worse than WHO's 1:1,000". |
| "India has over a million melanoma cases a year" | Wrong. The whole world had ~332,000 new melanoma cases in 2022. | Don't use it. |
| Indian skin cancer incidence | Skin cancer is **less common** in India than in Western countries. A judge may raise this. | Pitch Skin Cancer Detection on **late diagnosis**, the **dermatologist shortage** and **early-detection survival**, not on Indian case counts. |
| "Our AI is more accurate than doctors" | Your own model hasn't been clinically validated. | Say "studies show AI can match specialists" (Haenssle 2018) and "we're building a screening aid for doctors". |
| "$110 billion AI market" | That figure is **global**, and is sometimes misquoted as India's. | Label it "global" if used. India's AI-in-healthcare figure is $4.5B by 2030. |
| WHO "50% non-adherence" | Based on very old studies. | Use the 12.4% Mumbai figure instead, or say "WHO estimates about half". |
| Abridge 2026 valuations ($5.7B, $6B) | Unconfirmed tracker estimates. | Use the confirmed $5.3B (June 2025). |
